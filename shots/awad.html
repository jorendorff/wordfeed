irrupt

(i-RUHPT)

Friday, October 16, 2026

This week’s theme

Words that look like misspellings

Meaning

verb intr.:
1. To burst or break in.
2. (Of a population) To increase suddenly in number.

Etymology

From Latin irrumpere, from in- (into) + rumpere (to break). Earliest documented use: 1855.

Usage

“The odd stories of Yoko Ogawa, a Japanese author, irrupt into the ordinary world as if from the unconscious or the grave.”
Slightly Off; The Economist (London, UK); Jan 26, 2013.
