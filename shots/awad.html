izzard

(IZ-uhrd)

Friday, October 23, 2026

This week’s theme

Z in the spotlight

Meaning

noun: The letter Z.

Etymology

An alteration of earlier ezod/ezed, probably from French et zede (and zed), said at the end of the alphabet. Earliest documented use: c. 1726.

Usage

“He doesn’t know A from izzard when it comes to women.”
Dan Kennedy; Z; Edited by Meredith Broussard; The Encyclopedia of Exes; Crown; 2005.
