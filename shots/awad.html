priapean

(pry-uh-PEE-uhn)

Friday, October 30, 2026

This week’s theme

Eponyms from antiquity

Meaning

adjective:
1. Phallic.
2. Lewd; salacious.

Etymology

After Priapus, a god of fertility in Greek and Roman mythology. Earliest documented use: 1623.

Usage

“[Baby Doll] describes, with a degree of Priapean detail that might well have embarrassed Boccaccio, how the syndicate’s manager ... gets his revenge.”
New Picture; Time (New York); Dec 24, 1956.
