The adjectival "apart from each other" sense can be found in the phrase "poles asunder," used to describe two things that are as vastly far apart as the poles of the Earth. </p>

    <strong>fervid</strong> &#149; \FER-vid\&nbsp; &#149; <em>adjective</em><br />
    <p><em>Fervid</em> is used to describe people or things that express or are expressive of strong feelings.</p>

<p>// The core of the book is the <em>fervid</em> letters the friends exchanged during the years they were apart.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/fervid">See the entry ></a></p>

  <p>
    <strong>Did you know?</strong><br />
